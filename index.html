Common Questions

FAQ

I've never done CrossFit before, can I still join? +
Yes. Every workout can be adjusted to match your current fitness level, and our coaches will guide you every step of the way. No prior experience is required.
What does a CrossFit class look like? +
Our 1 hour classes are broken into 4 parts: Warm Up · Strength Focus (20 minutes to focus on 1 movement) · WOD — Workout of the day, a high intensity workout · Cool Down/Stretch.
What if I can't do some of the movements? +
Every workout can be modified for beginners through to experienced athletes. The goal is for everyone to get the right workout for them. Our coaches can help you scale or modify the workout for you.
Can I do a trial class before signing up? +
Yes. You can book a trial class before committing to a membership. The fee is $25 + HST. Once you've booked a class you'll receive an email on what to expect on your first day.
Are your coaches certified? +
Yes. Every one of our coaches is a certified CrossFit coach with a minimum certification of L1. Check out our coaches here.
I heard that people get injured from doing CrossFit. Is that true? +
We prioritize technique first. Coaches demonstrate movements, provide cues and corrections, and scale workouts appropriately so you can train safely and effectively.
What are the membership options? +
You can view our membership options here.
Is there a contract or sign-up fee? +
No. We do not charge any sign-up fee. Our memberships are month to month with no initiation commitment. If, at any time, you wish to cancel we require 2 weeks' notice.
Do you offer any discounts? +
Yes we do. We offer 10% off memberships for First Responders. We also offer 10% off for the 2nd, 3rd and 4th person in a family. Please contact us at hello@elementmvmt.com to apply the discount prior to registering.
Do you have showers or change rooms? +
Yes we do. Our facility has both changerooms and showers.
How often should I work out? +
We recommend working out a minimum of 3 times a week for optimum results.
What is the schedule of classes? +
We have multiple classes per weekday. You can see the schedule here.
What are the different types of classes? +
WOD — This 60 minute class is suitable for all fitness levels.
AD — This 90 minute class is for the advanced CrossFit athlete and only available with a Prime Membership.
Teens — This class is suitable for kids aged 10–16.
Open Gym — Allows access to the gym to do your own workout at any time other than when a class is being held. Only available with a Prime Membership.
