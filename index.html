Common Questions

FAQ

Is CrossFit for me? +
Yes. Regardless of age or fitness level, everyone can do CrossFit. The beauty of CrossFit is that it is endlessly modifiable. Our experienced coaches modify the program for each person to help you safely become healthier and fitter.
Do I need to be in shape to start? +
No. The best part of CrossFit is that you can modify every workout to your current fitness level. As you become stronger you can adjust the workouts to be more challenging. Contact us before booking your first class so we can set you up for success.
What should I expect on my first class? +
You may feel nervous — trying anything new can be unsettling — but our coaches will be there to ensure your first class is a great experience. Expect to be guided through every movement in a welcoming, non-judgmental environment.
What memberships are available? +
We offer Prime ($225/mo), Unlimited ($195/mo), Basic — 12 classes ($175/mo), and Open Gym ($175/mo). All memberships are month-to-month with no sign-up or cancellation fee. Drop-ins, class passes, and personal training are also available.
Do you offer discounts? +
Yes — First Responders receive 10% off memberships (proof of employment required), and family members receive 10% off for each additional member. Only one discount per member applies.
