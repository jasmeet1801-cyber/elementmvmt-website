How It Works

Three Steps to Day One

01
Choose a Membership
Browse our simple, month-to-month membership options below. No sign-up fee, no cancellation fee, no long-term commitment. Pick what works for your schedule and budget.
02
Register Online
Sign up through ZenPlanner — our member portal. It takes about 3 minutes. Once you're set up you can book classes, track your progress, and access SugarWOD.
03
Show Up & Move
Walk in, introduce yourself, and let your coach take it from there. Every class is led start to finish. You'll leave having moved well, worked hard, and met a few great people.

What to Expect

Your First Class

  • Show up 5–10 minutes early so your coach can introduce themselves and walk you through the space.
  • Every class starts with a coach-led warm-up — no experience needed to follow along.
  • Movements are always scaled to your current fitness level. Nobody lifts the same load on day one.
  • Coaches watch your form and give cues throughout. Your safety is always the priority.
  • After class, stick around — the community is one of the best parts of EMC.
  • Wear comfortable athletic wear and bring a water bottle. We have everything else.
