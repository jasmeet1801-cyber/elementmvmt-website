Before You Submit

Your regular billing is paused and replaced with the $25 hold fee for the duration.
Notice Required
At Least 2 Weeks
Hold requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and cannot be backdated.
Refunds
Non-Refundable
Any payments already billed prior to the hold start date are non-refundable.
